Hi, I'm Jinqi Gong, a graduate of the [University of Southern California](https://www.usc.edu/), where I earned my Master of Science in [Analytics (MS)](https://catalogue.usc.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=20&poid=28702&returnto=8266) from the [Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://ise.usc.edu/) at the [Viterbi School of Engineering](https://viterbischool.usc.edu/). My research interests include machine learning, AI Agents, data analysis, and mathematical modeling.

I earned my Bachelor of Science in [Mathematics Department](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/math/), specializing in [Statistics and Data Science](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/math/bsc-courses/bachelor-sds-from-2019/), from the Department of Mathematics at the [Faculty of Science and Technology](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/), [University of Macau](https://www.um.edu.mo/).

I was a visiting student at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/) in Spring 2023, and a research assistant at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/) - [Information Retrieval Lab at Tsinghua University (THUIR) ](http://www.thuir.cn/) - in Summer 2023.
